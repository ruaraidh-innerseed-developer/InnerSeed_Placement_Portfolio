# STAN — what it is and what it is for

**Superseded the previous strategy, 2026-09-15.** The earlier version treated
STAN as the business. It isn't. See `DECISIONS.md` for the settled position.

---

## The shape

**STAN is the public resource. The coaching practice is the business.**

STAN is an independent, evidence-governed resource for people affected by
anabolic steroid use: a question bank, marker pages, seminars, groups, and
crisis signposting. Free, no affiliate money, no advertising. Its job is to be
found, to be true, and to be trusted.

Ruaraidh works as a recovery coach specialising in **coming off**. That is what
earns. STAN makes it credible and brings people within reach of it.

Neither one pretends to be the other. Wherever STAN points at the coaching, it
says so plainly and shows other routes, including free ones. That rule is not
decoration — it is the only thing that lets both exist without the first
poisoning the second.

## Why this and not the alternatives

Every other model examined ran into one of two walls.

**The money in this field is paid for continuation.** Coaches charge £100–300 a
month and TRT clinics £99–200 a month, both paid monthly by someone who is
carrying on. A business that succeeds when people stop is structurally opposed
to where the money sits. Coaching people through stopping is the one model where
the payment arrives when the mission succeeds.

**Everything else needed people who don't exist yet.** Clinical referral needs a
panel. Groups need two trained facilitators, insurance and a crisis protocol.
Commissioning needs a track record. Coaching needs one trained person, which is
the only resource actually available.

## What the market looks like

Checked 2026-09-15, and it is not empty.

| | Price | Who for |
|---|---|---|
| NHS, needle exchange, IPED clinics | Free | Anyone, but patchy and thin |
| **Nothing** | **£50–500** | **—** |
| Private residential rehab | £18,000–40,000 for 28 days | People who accept the label "addict" |
| TRT clinic cessation plans | £100–200/month | Their own prescribed patients |

Castle Craig and others market steroid addiction treatment at roughly £650 a
day. Voy sells a tapering plan with monitoring. Demand is proven by people
already paying five figures.

The gap is the whole middle, and the rehab framing excludes most of the
population anyway — a man on a heavy dose who wants to stop does not think he is
an addict and will not walk into a clinic.

**This has not been validated with demand data.** Search volume, competitors on
Instagram, and forum language are all unchecked. See `DECISIONS.md`.

## What STAN is for, concretely

1. **Being findable.** The question bank answers things people search for and
   get nothing useful back on. That is the top of the funnel and the reason the
   site was rebuilt with real, indexable pages.
2. **Being trusted.** The claim discipline, the published limits, the refusal to
   guess. A coach with a governance apparatus behind him is not the same
   proposition as a coach with an Instagram account.
3. **Reaching people early.** Most arrive long before they are ready to pay for
   anything. The site's job is to be useful to them anyway.
4. **Accumulating the asset.** The unmet-question log now; outcomes later. That
   is what a researcher or commissioner eventually pays for.

## What STAN is not for

- Delivering a clinical service.
- Duplicating needle exchanges and IPED clinics that already exist.
- Running groups before there are trained facilitators, insurance and a crisis
  protocol. That is the highest-risk thing on the list, not the easiest.
- Generating revenue itself. It costs about £12 a year to run.

## Sequence

1. Split STAN into its own repository. Independent of InnerSeed.
2. Domain and email address. Gates everything. About £12.
3. Name the coaching service and decide what it includes. Not generic
   "recovery coaching" — specifically coming off.
4. Validate: keyword volume, competitors, forum language.
5. Coaching training, and insurance if the training requires it.
6. Site live, with the coaching disclosed and listed alongside other routes.
7. First clients. Learn what people actually need.

Steps 2 to 5 involve no engineering.

## The constraint that has not moved

Still one person. Everything above is deliverable solo except the groups and the
clinical work, which is why neither is in the first six steps. But nothing grows
past a single practice without a second human being involved, and that has been
true since the first week.
