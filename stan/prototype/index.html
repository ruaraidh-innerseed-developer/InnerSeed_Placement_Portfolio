Free · Online · Recorded where we can

Seminars

Someone who actually knows the subject talks for forty minutes, then answers whatever you ask. Recordings go into the library, so if the time you need it is three in the morning, it is there.

Free · Online · Cameras optional · No commitment

Groups

Not a forum and not a chat room. A room with a start time, a finish time, and two people holding it. Turn up and say nothing if you would rather — plenty of people do for the first few weeks.

The part that actually helps

Connecting people who have no clue with people who do

Two different kinds of knowing, and you need both. Neither one substitutes for the other, and we keep the line between them clear on purpose.

Peer — lived experience

Someone who has been where you are

Trained volunteers who have used, come off, and come out the other side. They will tell you what it was like and sit with you while it's hard.

  • Will share their own experience, openly
  • Will help you work out what to ask
  • Will not tell you what to take, or read your bloods

Clinical — registered professionals

Someone qualified to answer

Endocrinology, andrology and male fertility clinicians who understand supraphysiological exposure — not just age-related low testosterone.

  • Can interpret results and advise on treatment
  • Will review everything clinical before it goes live
  • Any fee STAN receives for a referral will be shown on that page

None appointed yet. Until one is, nothing that interprets or advises gets published here — only things we can report and attribute. We would rather say that than imply otherwise.

If tonight is bad

STAN is not a crisis service and nobody here is on call. These people are, and they are better at it than we are. Check the hours — two of these are not open around the clock.

Prototype. Nothing on this page has been clinically reviewed and the group sessions shown are examples, not real bookings. Crisis numbers are drawn from STAN's services register and are pending confirmation against each provider's own website.

STAN publishes general information. It does not assess, diagnose or prescribe, and it does not publish dosing protocols or tell anyone where to obtain anything.

How we work. Answers are drafted with AI and checked by people. Nothing clinical publishes without review. Every answer names its sources and shows what we have not read yet — and where the evidence does not exist, we say so rather than filling the gap.

Money. Reading is free. A person's time costs, and the whole list, including what does not exist yet, is on one page: what costs money here, and why.
