Being straight with you about this

You've probably already asked an AI about this

Almost everyone does now, and it isn't a bad instinct. We use AI too — these pages are drafted with it. We'd rather tell you that than let you assume otherwise.

What a chatbot is genuinely good at

Free, patient, awake at three in the morning, and it won't judge you or sigh when you ask the same thing six times. For plenty of questions that's exactly what you need.

What it can't do for this

  • Tell you when the answer doesn't exist. On recovery after long-term use, the honest answer is often "nobody has studied this". You'll get a confident one anyway.
  • Know whether the study fits you. Most testosterone research is about men in their sixties. You might be thirty-two and four years off 400mg a week.
  • Show its working, so you can check it.
  • Be corrected and stay corrected.
The difference isn't whether a machine was involved. It's what happens between the draft and you reading it — a named source for every claim, a record of what we haven't read yet, figures we refuse to print because we can't stand behind them, and someone's name on the page.

We can't promise you'll be safe. Nobody can, and anyone who does is selling something. We can make sure you're not guessing.

The part that actually helps

Connecting people who have no clue with people who do

Two different kinds of knowing, and you need both. Neither one substitutes for the other, and we keep the line between them clear on purpose.

Peer — lived experience

Someone who has been where you are

Trained volunteers who have used, come off, and come out the other side. They will tell you what it was like and sit with you while it's hard.

  • Will share their own experience, openly
  • Will help you work out what to ask
  • Will not tell you what to take, or read your bloods

Clinical — registered professionals

Someone qualified to answer

Endocrinology, andrology and male fertility clinicians who understand supraphysiological exposure — not just age-related low testosterone.

  • Can interpret results and advise on treatment
  • Reviews everything published here before it goes live
  • Any fee STAN receives for a referral is shown on the page
